About Me

I'm a B.Tech Computer Engineering student at CHARUSAT University, and the part of this field that pulls me in the most is AI, Machine Learning and Data Science. I also have a strong foundation in Data Structures & Algorithms, which has strengthened my problem-solving, logical thinking, and ability to approach complex problems systematically. I like the full loop: take a raw dataset, find what's actually in it, build a model on top, and put it in front of real users.

That loop is reflected in my projects. In the Netflix Content Analytics & Recommendation System, I analysed 8,000+ titles and built a content-based recommender using TF-IDF and cosine similarity. I've also built Cortex, a Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) research assistant for working with documents and extracting relevant information. Alongside this, I have worked on a space-focused AI project that combines my interest in AI with real-world problem solving.

Underneath it all is a strong base in Python, C++ and Data Structures & Algorithms, which keeps my code clean and my thinking structured.
